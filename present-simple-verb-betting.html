How to Play

  • A subject + verb appears — place your bet!
  • No Change = I / you / we / they + verb stays the same
  • +S = he / she / it + most verbs just add -s
  • +ES = he / she / it + verbs ending in ch, sh, or o
  • Irregular = have, do, go → has, does, goes (they break the pattern!)
  • Correct bet = +1 point  |  Wrong = -1 point
