Listening Dictation

AR & OR Sounds

After each sentence, students write the AR/OR words they heard on their own list — then the next slide shows the class chart updated with those words, so they can check themselves. Ends with 2 "gotcha" spelling-trap slides and a preview of the next lesson.

Read each sentence aloud (twice, slowly). Give students time to write before advancing to the chart slide. You can also click any word on a sentence slide to check it individually — the true color only shows once clicked.

OLIVE — ar sound ORANGE — or sound PURPLE — er sound (next lesson) black — not a target sound
